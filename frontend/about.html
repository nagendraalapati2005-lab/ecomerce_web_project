Our Story

Franki's Pizzeria started in 20XX with one simple idea: make the pizza we would want to eat ourselves. Today we still make our dough fresh, build every pizza by hand, and bake it only after you order. Whether it is a quick dinner or a night with friends, we want every box to feel like it was made just for you.
